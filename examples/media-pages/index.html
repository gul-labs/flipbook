Media pages

The engine never plays or pauses your media. media.ts does, from the engine's events: sound pauses when a turn starts, resumes if the turn is abandoned, and everything on a page that leaves the screen stops. Muted loops start when their page arrives (not under reduced motion). See docs/MEDIA-PAGES.md.
